# Hydra Icon
*Wondrous item, very rare*

**Price:** 30,000-60,000 gp | medium sentimentality

This icon is a sandstone statuette of a hydra with five heads. Red swirls within its grain seem to divide and grow if you examine them closely. It can grant Aspect of Hydra for 10 minutes. Once it has been used, it can't be used again until 5 days have passed. 

**Aspect of Hydra.** Your form seems to split into multiple transparent copies, as though someone is looking at you without focusing their eyes on you. If you suffer damage equal to or greater than a fifth of your maximum hit points during a creature's turn, you can use a reaction to regain all hit points you lost at the end of that creature's turn unless you suffered fire damage during that turn. You are vulnerable to fire damage.

## Paid

The icon can be used again after 2 days, instead of 5 days.

## Overpaid

Aspect of Hydra lasts 1 hour instead of 10 minutes.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
