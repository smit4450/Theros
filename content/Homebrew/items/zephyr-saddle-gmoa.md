# Zephyr Saddle
*Wondrous item, rare*

**Price:** 1,000-6,000 gp | low sentimentality

This saddle is shaped into an artistic representation of a gust of wind. A temperate breeze flows around you when you sit on it.

## Paid

While a horse wears the saddle, the horse gains +20 hit points, +4 AC, and +10 feet of speed.

## Overpaid

While using the saddle to ride a horse, you gain a +4 bonus to all checks to stay on the horse.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
