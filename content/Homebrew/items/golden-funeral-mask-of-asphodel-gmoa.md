# Golden Funeral Mask of Asphodel
*Wondrous item, common*
![Golden Funeral Mask of Asphodel](Homebrew/items/img/golden-funeral-mask-of-asphodel.webp#right)
**Price:** 1-5 cp | low sentimentality

The expression on this mask is eerily neutral, so unfeeling you wonder what thoughts lurk underneath. While you wear the mask, you don't feel anxiety.

## Paid

While you wear this mask, you are immune to the [[conditions#Frightened|frightened]] condition.

## Overpaid

You learn the location of the necropolis of Asphodel and become familiar with the layout of its streets.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
