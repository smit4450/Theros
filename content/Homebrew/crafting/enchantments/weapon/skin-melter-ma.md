# Skin Melter
*Weapon Enchantment, Rare (requires attunement)*

When you hit a creature with this weapon it takes an extra 1d10 acid damage and is [[conditions#Frightened|Frightened]] of you until the end of its next turn.

*Source: Mystic Arts*
