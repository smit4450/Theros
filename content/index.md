# Welcome to Theros

*The caprice of the gods. The destiny of heroes. Great journeys into monster-infested wilds, and mythic odysseys to the edge of the world and back.*

Welcome, hero. This wiki is your companion throughout our campaign in **Theros** — a world rooted in the myths of ancient Greece, where gods walk among mortals, heroes defy fate, and legends are forged in the fires of divine ambition. Use this guide to look up rules, explore lore, plan your character, and discover the wonders and dangers of this mythic land.

## Piety & Divine Rewards

Devotion to the gods grants supernatural blessings. As your piety increases, you gain access to greater rewards.

Each god offers three tiers of supernatural reward:
- **Votary** — an initiate's blessing
- **Disciple** — a proven servant's gift
- **Devotee** — a true champion's power

Additionally, your piety may grant you the title of **Champion**, earning one of fifteen legendary boons:
