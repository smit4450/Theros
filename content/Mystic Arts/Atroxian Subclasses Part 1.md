# Atroxian Subclasses
> And so the Emperor proclaimed to his people: "Let the Old World keep their knights-errant, their priests and shamans. We don't need them. There's three things that make Atroxia great: Steel, wealth and gunpowder."
> At the moment I felt a nudge on my shoulder. It was Carl Moore. "Damn right. You heard the grand colonizer: steal wealth with gunpowder."

Atroxia is a diverse place, where any combination of species, class and subclass could be found. But players wanting to lean into the setting's rich flintlock-fantasy flavor might consider playing an Atroxian subclass.  In this issue we present four such subclasses:

- The Archaeologist (Ranger)
- The Collegiate (Wizard)
- The Fundamental (Sorcerer)
- The Mage Hunter (Fighter) 

### Archaeologist Ranger
*Unearth the Continent's Hidden Treasures*

> “I'll be frank, Barton. I've got no idea what we do now. I'm making this up as I go.”
> - Professor Henk Vilgar, Philosopher of Ethnolinguistics

You're a two-fisted pulp hero with a knack for understanding the world of ages past. When trouble inevitably comes knocking, you'll knock them back and ensure artefacts end up in a museum where they belong.

#### Level 3: Scrappy Tactics
Understanding your competition grants you an edge in a scrap. You gain the following benefits:

**Good Right Hook.** You can make an unarmed strike as a bonus action.
**Studious Scrapper.** When you attempt to grapple or shove a creature that is marked by your Hunter's Mark, the target subtracts 1d6 from its saving throw.
**Grit and Gumption.** When you cast Hunter's Mark, you gain temporary hit points equal to 1d10 plus your Ranger level.

#### Level 3: Archaeologist Spells
When you reach a Ranger level specified in the Archaeologist Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

#### Level 3: Eye for Treasure
You can cast the Identify spell as an action without expending a spell slot. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

When you use this feature to identify a magic item that requires attunement and you are capable of attuning to, you can attune to the item as part of the same action. 

Once you use this feature to attune to a magic item, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

#### Level 7: Duck & Deck 'Em
When a creature hits you with an attack, you can take a reaction to increase your AC against that attack by an amount equal to your proficiency bonus, potentially causing it to miss.

If you have a creature grappled when you take this reaction, the bonus to AC instead lasts until the start of your next turn or until you are no longer grappling that creature. 

While this bonus is active, each time another creature misses you with an attack, the grappled creature takes damage of that attack's type equal to your proficiency bonus. 

If it is marked by your Hunter's Mark, it takes 1d6 additional force damage.

#### Level 11: Superior Scrapper
When you use your scrappy tactics feature to make an unarmed strike as a bonus action, you may make two unarmed strikes instead. 
You may replace one of these unarmed strikes with a casting of Hunter's Mark, or with moving your Hunter's Mark to a new creature if you previously reduced the current target to 0 hit points (as per the spell's description).

#### Level 15: Lucky Mark
You gain the following features. When you use either of them, your Hunter's Mark ends.

**Lucky Hit.** When you hit a creature marked with your Hunter's Mark with an attack, you can turn that hit into a critical hit instead.

**Lucky Dodge.** When a creature marked with your Hunter's Mark hits you with an attack, you can take a reaction to cause the attack to miss instead, then make an unarmed strike against the attacking creature if it is within your reach.

### Collegiate Wizard
*Leadership Through Arcane Brilliance*

> "You don't get it, do you? They all cast spells! They all went to the University! Reynolds, Powell, Klein, Krauser, Rowe, Rosenthal. They run the country, but they're old school mates. It's one big club, and we're not invited."
> - Bridget Kincaid, Police Constable and Revolutionary

Like Presidents, Chancellors, Judges, and anyone else who matters in the affairs of state. You were educated at the Arthusian University: the nation's greatest and highest institution for learning. There, you were taught how to cast spells and how to lead commoners.

#### Level 3: Agents of the Arcane
Whenever you finish a long rest, you can use calligrapher’s supplies to create one or more Writs of Appointment. 

You can have a number of writs at a time equal to your proficiency bonus. If creating a writ would cause you to exceed that number, you must choose an older writ to be destroyed.

**Signed Writs.** A Writ of Appointment has no effect until it is signed by a willing creature other than you. Once a creature signs the writ, they become your agent while they carry the writ on their person. While an agent is on the same plane of existence as you, you always know their distance and direction from you, and whether they are affected by any conditions, are below their hit point maximum, or are at 0 hit points.

**Arcane Relay.** When you cast a Wizard spell with a range of touch, one of your agents can deliver the touch. The agent must be on the same plane of existence as you, and it must take a Reaction to deliver the touch when you cast the spell. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

#### Level 3: Honors Graduate
Choose one skill in which you have proficiency but lack expertise. You gain expertise with that skill.

You also gain proficiency with calligrapher's supplies. If you already have this tool proficiency, you gain proficiency with one other type of artisan’s tools of your choice.

#### Level 6: Codified Spellcraft
While you have calligrapher's supplies and your spellbook in hand, you can spend some time creating a Codified Spellscroll. 
Pick a spell from your spellbook that meets the following criteria:

- It has a range other than 'Self'.
- It does not require an attack roll or save.
- It does not consume a material component.
- It does not require concentration.

The time it takes to create the scroll is equal to its casting time plus ten minutes. Once the Codified Spellscroll has been created, you or any of your agents can cast the spell inscribed on it as an action, destroying the scroll.

The scroll lasts until it is used, but you can only have a number of Codified Spellscrolls equal to your proficiency bonus. If creating a new one would cause you to exceed that number, you must choose an older scroll to be destroyed.

#### Level 10: Academic Composure
Long years of learning proper composure grant you the following features:

**Elementary.** Taking damage can't cause you to lose concentration on spells of 3rd level or lower.

**Risk Management.** If you fail on an Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma saving throw while you're concentrating on a spell, you can choose to lose concentration on that spell to succeed instead.

#### Level 14: Nexus of Authority
While an agent is on the same plane of existence as you, you can communicate with them telepathically, and they are considered to be a 'linked object' for the purposes of casting Teleport to their location.

**Eyes and Ears.** As a bonus action, you can see through an agent's eyes and hear what they hear until the start of your next turn, gaining the benefits of any special senses the agent has.

While you're seeing through the eyes of an agent who is within 100 feet of you, you gain the following effects:

* Once per turn, when they fail on a d20 test, the agent can add your Intelligence modifier to the roll, potentially turning it into a success.
* You can cast spells as though you were in the agent's space.

### Fundamental Sorcerer
*Understand the Weave. Alter the Weave.*

> "What right do you have?" demanded the Special Agent, wielding their leadwave. "You're no different than the rest of us, there are laws in this country! Laws you disregard because you think you're above them."
> "Spoken like the puppet you are," responded the Fundamentalist; sure of their footing. "I shouldn't judge, we're all puppets after all. I'm merely a puppet who can tug at the strings."

Atroxian law mandates all residents who can cast spells be documented by the Spellcaster Registry Service, a government organization meant to curtail the growth of the arcane.

For some, this registration is a restriction upon their fundamental rights. For others, they serve other fundamentals. They can see the weave, the fabric of the multiverse, and manipulate it according to their own desire. 

To make and unmake anything.

#### Level 3: Growing Understanding
When one or more creatures succeed on a saving throw against a spell you cast with a spell slot or one of your sorcerer class features, roll 1d4. 

Those creatures subtract the result of the roll from the next saving throw they make against a spell you cast or one of your sorcerer class features, before the end of your next turn.

#### Level 3: Fundamental Spells
When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the Fundamental Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

#### Level 6: Restructure Matter
As a Magic action, choose a creature or loose nonmagical object you can see within 30 feet of you and expend a number of Sorcery Points up to half your proficiency bonus (rounded down). You attempt to shape-shift that creature into a cloud of dispersed matter.

A shape-shifted creature can’t move or take actions, and other creatures can freely enter and occupy its space. It has immunity to all damage, as well as the grappled, restrained and prone conditions. 

When the effect ends, the target reforms in its space, or in the nearest unoccupied space if that space is occupied. You cannot target that creature with this effect again until you finish a long rest.

Pick one of the following effects when you use this feature:

**Disperse.** The target must make a Constitution saving throw against your spellcasting DC. On a failed save, it takes 1d10 force damage for each Sorcery Point you spent and shape-shifts until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage only.

**Consolidate.** If the target is a willing creature, it gains 1d10 temporary hit points for each Sorcery Point you spent and shape-shifts until the start of its next turn.

#### Level 14: Force Multiplier
As an action, you spend up to 5 sorcery points to create a perfect replica of yourself in an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet of you. The replica is intangible and doesn’t occupy its space. It lasts for a number of rounds equal to the number of sorcery points you spent creating it.

At the end of each of your turns, you may move the replica up to 30 feet and use Restructure Matter as if you were in the replica's space (no action required). When using Restructure Matter in this way, you must still use your own senses, and you can only choose the Disperse option. This use of Restructure Matter does not cost you any sorcery points, and deals 1d10 damage.

#### Level 18: Fundamental Being
When you use Innate Sorcery, you can alter it to infuse yourself with the essence of fundamental force, gaining the following benefits while this use of Innate Sorcery is active. 

Once you use this feature to alter Innate Sorcery, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest unless you spend 5 sorcery points (no action required) to restore your use of it.

**Flight.** You gain a fly (hover) speed of 60 ft.
**Perfected Perception.** You gain True Sight to a range of 60 feet.
**Mind Over Matter.** You can use Restructure Matter as a bonus action. When you do, it costs no sorcery points and deals 5d10 damage if you choose the Disperse option, or gives 5d10 temporary hit points if you choose the Consolidate option.

### Mage Hunter Fighter
*Counter Magic with an Arsenal of Gadgets*

> "Make your move, sorcerer," the mage hunter taunted, readying their leadwave. "It won't work, but it's only fair you go first."

The Bureau of Mage Enforcement trains experts in countering dangerous spellcasters. While they train with guns and swords like other warriors, their primary weapons are the gadgets they use to make magic obsolete.

#### Level 3: Specialist Gadgets
You learn to create various pieces of specialized equipment for dealing with different opponents.

**Schematics.** You learn three schematics of your choice from the "Mage Hunter Gadgets" section. You learn two additional schematics of your choice when you reach fighter levels 7, 10, and 15. Each time you learn new schematics, you can also replace a schematic you know with a different one.

**Preparing Gadgets.** When you finish a long rest you can use tinker's tools to prepare a number of gadgets from schematics you know, up to your proficiency bonus. A prepared gadget is a tiny item that weighs 1 lb. You can prepare multiple copies of the same gadget, but you can’t have more gadgets prepared in total than your proficiency bonus. If preparing a gadget causes you to exceed this limit, the oldest prepared gadget stops working entirely.

**Trained Hands**. Only you, or a creature you have spent a minute instructing, can use the gadgets you prepare with this feature, and anyone doing somust have a free hand available. Each gadget you prepare can only be used once before needing to be recharged. When you finish a short rest, you can recharge any number of your prepared gadgets that you have on your person.

**Saving Throws.** If a gadget you prepare with this feature requires a saving throw, the DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier.

#### Level 3: Working Knowledge
You gain the following features:

**Arcana Proficiency.** You gain proficiency in the arcana skill. If you already have this proficiency, you gain proficiency with one other skill of your choice.

**Tinker's Tools Proficiency.** You gain proficiency with tinker's tools. If you already have this tool proficiency, you gain proficiency with one other type of artisan’s tools of your choice.

#### Level 7: Brace the Mind
You gain proficiency in Wisdom saving throws. If you already have this proficiency, you instead gain proficiency in Intelligence or Charisma saving throws (your choice).

#### Level 10: Field Adjustments
By spending 1 minute working with your tinker's tools, you can recharge all of your currently prepared gadgets. As part of the same process, you can also choose one of your prepared gadgets and swap it out for a different one from a schematic you know. Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.

#### Level 15: Explosive Deployment
You can trigger a volatile reaction in a gadget, overloading it to produce a powerful explosion. When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks with overloading and throwing one of your prepared gadgets. Choose one prepared gadget and a point within 30 feet of you. 

Creatures within a 20 foot radius centered on that point must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 5d8 force damage. A creature that succeeds its saving throw takes half as much damage only. The gadget you chose no longer counts as prepared.

#### Level 18: Resourceful
Any time you roll initiative, you can recharge up to two of your prepared gadgets.


### Mage Hunter Equipment
Gadgets are presented in alphabetical order.

#### Crackleball
*Prerequisite: Level 3 Mage Hunter Fighter*

When you take the attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a crackleball; a small ball of bright copper, loaded with a powerful static charge. 

Choose a point on the ground within 30 feet of yourself. Creatures within a 10 foot radius centered on that point must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 lightning damage and be unable to take reactions until the start of their next turn. 

A creature that succeeds its saving throw takes half as much damage only.

#### Gnomeglass Goggles
*Prerequisite: Level 3 Mage Hunter Fighter*

As an action, you apply a drop of quicksilver to the glass of these hefty goggles. 

For the next hour, a creature wearing these goggles can see a faint aura around any visible creature or object in the area that bear any kind of magic, and if an effect was created by a spell, they learn the spell’s school of magic.

#### Gust Canister
*Prerequisite: Level 7 Mage Hunter Fighter*

As a reaction, taken when you or a creature within 5 feet of you are targeted by a ranged attack or forced to make a Dexterity saving throw, you release a burst of pressurized air from this metal canister to push back against the triggering effect. 

**Gust of Air.** Until the end of the current turn, you and all creatures in a 5-foot emanation orginating from you gain the benefits of three-quarters cover against any attacks or effects originating from outside the emanation. If a creature in the emanation succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw against an effect that would deal half damage on a success, they instead take no damage.

#### Hookline Sinker
*Prerequisite: Level 7 Mage Hunter Fighter*

When you take the attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with firing a hookline at a creature or a point on a solid surface within 30 feet of you that is not behind total cover. 

**Move You.** If fired at a point or a creature that is huge or larger, you may immediately move up to 30 feet directly towards the target without provoking opportunity attacks. 

**Move Them.** If fired at a creature that is large or smaller, that creature must succeed on a Strength saving throw or fall prone and be pulled up to 25 feet directly towards you.

#### Keymarker
*Prerequisite: Level 7 Mage Hunter Fighter*

As a reaction, taken when a creature you can see within 60 feet of you teleports or is transported to a different plane, you can activate this lodestone key. 

**Teleport.** If the triggering effect was a teleport, then for the next hour, the key will always point in the direction of the teleport's destination and vibrate with a frequency that depends on distance. For the duration, it counts as a linked object for the purposes of casting Teleport to that destination. 

**Planar Travel.** If the triggering effect transported the creature to a different plane, then for the next hour the key will resonate as if it were a forked, metal rod attuned to that plane of existence. While resonating in this way, the key can be used as a material component for plane shift, but if used in this way the target destination is always the same as the one specified by the triggering effect.

#### Lodestone Bolas
*Prerequisite: Level 10 Mage Hunter Fighter*

When you take the attack action, you can replace one of the attacks by throwing lodestone bolas. 
Target a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have the restrained condition until it or another creature within 5 feet of it takes an action to remove the bolas. While restrained in this way, the target cannot teleport.
The target succeeds automatically if its size is Huge or larger.

#### Moonflare
*Prerequisite: Level 3 Mage Hunter Fighter*

You can light this handflare as an action, causing it to become the origin of a 20-foot emanation for 10 minutes that has the following effects.

**Silvery Light.** The emanation is filled with a dim silvery light, that illuminates even magical darkness. 

**Revealing Light.** Invisible creatures and objects are visible as long as they are within the emanation. 

**Moon Light.** Shape-shifted creature that starts its turn within the emanation or enters it for the first time on a turn must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it reverts to its true form and can’t shape-shift until it leaves the emanation.

#### Nimrod
*Prerequisite: Level 3 Mage Hunter Fighter*

As an action, you drive this metal stake with a lodestone cap into the ground at a point within 5 feet of you, activating it for 1 hour. 
While the nimrod is active, the first time a creature that is within 60 feet of it and does not have total cover from it casts a spell, the nimrod becomes the target of that spell or attack instead. 

If the spell requires the caster to choose a point or a space, the chosen point or space becomes the one the nimrod occupies instead. 

#### Numbskull Brace
*Prerequisite: Level 3 Mage Hunter Fighter*

As a bonus action, you tap the side of this slim neckbrace, causing the needles lining its inside to extend into your flesh, dulling your nervous system. 

**Numbed Skull.** You become immune to the charmed and frightened conditions for 1 minute. If you are already charmed or frightened, those conditions are suppressed for the duration. 

**Using it On You.** A creature you have spent 1 minute instructing can trigger your numbskull brace, but doing so takes an action.

#### Quick-Release Leadwave
*Prerequisite: Level 10 Mage Hunter Fighter*

As a reaction, taken when a creature you can see within 60 feet of you begins casting a spell, you yank on the pullstring attached to this sectioned horseshoe-shaped device, causing it to emit a short, high-frequency shriek. 

Countering Spells. The triggering creature must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target is stunned until the start of its next turn, and the spell it was attempting to cast dissipates with no effect. If the spell was cast with a spell slot, the slot isn’t expended.

#### Redrod
*Prerequisite: Level 10 Mage Hunter Fighter*

As an action, you drive this metal stake with a thin bloodmetal cap into the ground at a point within 5 feet of you, activating it for 1 hour. Whenever a creature within 60 feet of an active redrod casts a spell, it takes 1d4 psychic damage per level of that spell (minimum 1d4).

#### Shocklock
*Prerequisite: Level 10 Mage Hunter Fighter*

When you take the attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with attempting to snap this jagged iron collar around the neck of a creature within 5 feet of you. 

That creature must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or be unable to speak until the collar is removed. The target succeeds automatically if it is Large or larger.

**Removing the Collar.** Removing the collar requires the creature wearing it or another creature within 5 feet of that creature to take an action and succeed on a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check with thieves' tools against your gadget DC. Each time the check is failed the creature wearing the collar takes 2d8 lightning damage.

#### Smotherball
*Prerequisite: Level 3 Mage Hunter Fighter*

When you take the attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a smotherball; a small ball of dull iron, loaded with smokepowder. 

Choose a point on the ground within 30 feet of yourself. A 20-foot radius sphere centered on that point becomes heavily obscured with smoke for 1 minute or until a strong wind disperses the smoke.

#### Sparkleball
*Prerequisite: Level 3 Mage Hunter Fighter*

When you take the attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a sparkleball; a small ball of bright brass, loaded with flashpowder. 

Choose a point on the ground within 30 feet of yourself. Creatures within a 10 foot radius centered on that point must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the end of their next turn.

#### Sustained Leadwave
*Prerequisite: Level 7 Mage Hunter Fighter*

As a bonus action, you can activate this weighty, horseshoe-shaped device, causing it to become the origin of a 20-foot emanation for 1 minute. 

**Break Concentration.** A creature that starts its turn within the emanation or enters it for the first time on a turn must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it loses concentration on any spells it's concentrating on, and cannot cast spells until the start of its next turn. 

**Throw the Device.** When a creature holding this item takes the attack action, it can replace one of its attacks with throwing the leadwave to a point on the ground a number of feet away up to twice its Strength score.
