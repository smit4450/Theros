# Undying Asphodel
*Wondrous item, common*

**Price:** 1-5 cp | no sentimentality

The asphodel flowers on this sprig never wither, fall off, or otherwise suffer aging effects, appearing fresh as the day they were plucked.

## Paid

The sprig and its flowers are immune to all damage.

## Overpaid

The flowers don't trigger allergies.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
