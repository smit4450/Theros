# Alchemist's Fire
*Adventuring gear*  


- **Cost**: 50 gp
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

When you take the [[actions#Attack|Attack]] action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a flask of Alchemist's Fire. Target one creature or object you can see within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and [[proficiency-xphb|Proficiency Bonus]]) or take `1d4` Fire damage and start [[burning-xphb|burning]].

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 222. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
