# Clay Owl
*Wondrous item, uncommon*

**Price:** 90-8,000 gp | low sentimentality

This cute sculpture has eyes a tad too wide even for an owl. It hoots when you squeeze it.

## Paid

The first time it hoots each day, creatures within 30 feet that its eyes are facing must succeed a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or be compelled to speak their true name.

## Overpaid

You can choose up to 3 hoots a day to attempt to compel creatures to speak their true name, instead of the first time each day. Cap of Authority Bundle of Chapparal Grass Chorus Mask Conspirator's Whistle

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
