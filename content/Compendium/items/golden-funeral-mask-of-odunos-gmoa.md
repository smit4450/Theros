# Golden Funeral Mask of Odunos
*Wondrous item, uncommon*

**Price:** 100-250 gp | low sentimentality

The expression on this mask is twisted in anger. While you wear the mask, you feel great rage. You gain a +1 bonus to damage rolls.

## Paid

While you wear the mask, the Returned of Odunos don't attack you.

## Overpaid

You learn the location of the necropolis of Odunos and become familiar with the layout of its streets.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
