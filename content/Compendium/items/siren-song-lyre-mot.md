# Siren Song Lyre
*Wondrous item, instrument, rare (requires attunement)*  



You can use an action to play this lyre and cast one of the following spells from it: [[animal-friendship-xphb|animal friendship]], [[charm-person-xphb|charm person]], [[enthrall-xphb|enthrall]], [[suggestion-xphb|suggestion]]. If the spell requires a saving throw, the spell save DC is 13.

Once the instrument has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn.

*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 198*
