# Chapparal Grass
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  



*Price: 50-100 gp | no sentimentality*

This bundle of dry, stubby grass from the chapparal between Setessa and the Nessian Wilds is packed with nutrients and water. It can feed 1 horse or similar pack animal and sustain it for 10 days without the need for additional nourishment.

## Paid

You receive 10 bundles of grass.

## Overpaid

You receive 20 bundles of grass.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
