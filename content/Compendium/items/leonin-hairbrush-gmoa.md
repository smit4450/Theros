# Leonin Hairbrush
*Wondrous item, common*  



*Price: 5-20 gp | no sentimentality*

This brush comes from Oreskos, a remote river valley ruled by leonin where grooming is important for cleanliness.

## Paid

The hairbrush effortlessly and painlessly untangles knots.

## Overpaid

You attract domesticated animals that have fur in need of brushing. The hairbrush loses this property if you directly or indirectly cause harm to an animal that approaches you for this reason.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
