# Pig Icon
*Wondrous item, uncommon*

**Price:** 100-500 gp | low sentimentality

This icon is a cherry wood statuette of a pig rooting for food. It can grant Aspect of Pig for 1 hour. Once it has been used, it can't be used again until 3 days have passed. 

**Aspect of Pig.** Your nose turns into a pig's snout and anything edible smells and tastes appetizing. You have [advantage](Compendium/rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on Wisdom ([Perception](Compendium/rules/skills.md#Perception)) checks that rely on smell and are resistant to poison damage. You're immune to the [poisoned](Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition.

## Paid

The icon doesn't require attunement.

## Overpaid

Aspect of Pig lasts 4 hours instead of 1 hour.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
