# Peacock Icon
*Wondrous item, rare*

**Price:** 2,000-3,500 gp | medium sentimentality

This icon is a metal statuette made from some unknown alloy that gives it an iridescent, rainbow effect, and its form is of a male peacock with feathers raised. It can grant Aspect of Peacock for 3 hours. Once it has been used, it can't be used again until 3 days have passed. 

**Aspect of Peacock.** You're able to see in all directions and have [advantage](Compendium/rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on Wisdom ([Perception](Compendium/rules/skills.md#Perception)) checks that rely on sight. This effect persists even if you are asleep or unconscious unless you use a [bonus action](Compendium/rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md) to disable this effect.

## Paid

The icon can be used again after 2 days, instead of 3 days.
