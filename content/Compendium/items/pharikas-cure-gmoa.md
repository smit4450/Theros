# Pharika's Cure
*Potion, uncommon*
![[Pharikas Mender.webp]]
**Price:** 10-150 gp | no sentimentality

When you drink this potion, roll a d4. If you roll a 1, you suffer poison damage equal to your maximum hit points. The potion's smoky, purplish-pink liquid hisses like a pit of snakes.

## Paid

When you drink this potion, you gain the benefits of the [[greater-restoration-xphb|greater restoration]] spell. You must still roll to see if you suffer poison damage.

## Overpaid

You roll a d10 instead of a d4 to determine if you suffer poison damage.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
