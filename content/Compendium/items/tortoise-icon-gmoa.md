# Tortoise Icon
*Wondrous item, rare*  



*Price: 2,000-3,500 gp | medium sentimentality*

This copper statuette is shaped like a turtle. It can grant Aspect of Turtle for 1 minute. Once it has been used, it can't be used again until 7 days have passed.

**Aspect of Turtle.** The clothing you're wearing gains the coloration and texture of a turtle's shell. You can use an action to target one creature you saw within the last round (6 seconds) and choose whether you want the creature to fall [[conditions#Unconscious|unconscious]] or have its speed halved. The target must succeed a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or succumb to the chosen effect for 5 rounds (30 seconds) or until they take damage.

## Paid

The icon can be used again after 4 days, instead of 7 days.

## Overpaid

Aspect of Turtle lasts 10 minutes instead of 1 minute.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
