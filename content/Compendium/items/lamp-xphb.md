# Lamp
*Adventuring gear*  
![](Compendium/items/img/lamp.webp#right)

- **Cost**: 5 sp
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

A Lamp burns [[oil-xphb|Oil]] as fuel to cast [[bright-light-xphb|Bright Light]] in a 15-foot radius and [[dim-light-xphb|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet.
