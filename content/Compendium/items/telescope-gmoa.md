# Telescope
*Common*  



*Price: 10 gp | no sentimentality*

This telescope allows you to get a closer look at the constellations in the night sky.

## Paid

When you point the telescope at a constellation, you learn the story behind that god or hero.

## Overpaid

You can see the stars and constellations when you peer through the telescope even if it's daytime or cloudy.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
