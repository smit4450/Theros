# Ring of Invisibility
*Ring, legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](/Compendium/items/img/ring-of-invisibility.webp#right)


While wearing this ring, you can take a [Magic](/Compendium/rules/actions.md#Magic) action to give yourself the [Invisible](/Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) condition. You remain [Invisible](/Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) until the ring is removed or until you take a [Bonus Action](/Compendium/rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md) to become visible again.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
