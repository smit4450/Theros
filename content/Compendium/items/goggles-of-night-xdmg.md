# Goggles of Night
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![](Compendium/items/img/goggles-of-night.webp#right)


While wearing these dark lenses, you have [[senses#Darkvision|Darkvision]] out to 60 feet. If you already have [[senses#Darkvision|Darkvision]], wearing the goggles increases its range by 60 feet.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 265. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
