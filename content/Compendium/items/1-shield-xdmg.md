# +1 Shield
*Armor ([[shield-xphb|shield]]), uncommon*  


- **Armor Class**: 2
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

While holding this Shield, you have a +1 bonus to [[armor-class-xphb|Armor Class]], in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC.
