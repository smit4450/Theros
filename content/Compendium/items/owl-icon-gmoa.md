# Owl Icon
*Wondrous item, uncommon*

**Price:** 200-750 gp | low sentimentality

This icon is an oak wood statuette of a perched owl. It can grant Aspect of Owl for 1 hour. Once it has been used, it can't be used again until 24 hours have passed. 

**Aspect of Owl.** Your eyes become wider and rounder. You have darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. If you already have darkvision, its range increases 60 feet. You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

## Paid

The icon doesn't require attunement.

## Overpaid

Aspect of Owl lasts 2 hours instead of 1 hour.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
