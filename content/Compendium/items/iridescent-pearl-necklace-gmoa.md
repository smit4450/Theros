# Iridescent Pearl Necklace
*Wondrous item, common*  



*Price: 50-100 gp | low sentimentality*

The pearls of this necklace are said to be the tears of a dead and forgotten god of love. While you wear this necklace, a light breeze causes your hair and clothing to billow elegantly while your face is always partially illuminated by soft and romantic light. Humanoids perceive you as beautiful but sad. This necklace consists of 85 pearls and merchants generally pay 1 gp for each pearl or 100 gp for the intact necklace.

## Paid

While wearing this necklace, you can use a bonus action to disable its effects until dawn the next day.

## Overpaid

Once a day, while wearing this necklace, you can use an action to target one creature and know if it is experiencing great love or grief.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
