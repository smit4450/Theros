# Scroll of Protection (Oozes)
*Scroll, rare*  
![](Compendium/items/img/scroll-of-protection.webp#right)


Using a [[actions#Magic|Magic]] action to read the scroll creates a 5-foot [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]] originating from you. For 5 minutes, {{item.detail2}} can't enter or affect anything in the area. However, if you move in such a way that an Ooze would be inside the area, the effect ends.

As a [[actions#Magic|Magic]] action, a creature within 5 feet of the [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]] can attempt to overcome it, which forces the creature to make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a successful save, the creature ceases to be affected by the [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]].

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 302*
