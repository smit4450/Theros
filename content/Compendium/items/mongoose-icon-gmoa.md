# Mongoose Icon
*Wondrous item, legendary*

**Price:** 200,000-500,000 gp | high sentimentality

This icon is an ethereal statuette of swirling mists that resemble a dashing mongoose, though it feels solid and cold to the touch. It can grant Aspect of Mongoose for 1 minute. Once it has been used, it can't be used again until 6 days have passed. 

**Aspect of Mongoose.** Your form elongates and you look sleek and transparent. You are immune to all spell effects.

## Paid

The icon can be used again after 3 days, instead of 6 days.

## Overpaid

Aspect of Mongoose lasts 10 minutes instead of 1 minute.

*Source: The Gray Merchant of Asphodel*
