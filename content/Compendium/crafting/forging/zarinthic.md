# Zarinthic
*Rare Metal, Rare (1,000 gp per ingot)*

The sahuagin mastered the craft of working with Zarinthic, a metal that naturally occurs in the deep trenches of the ocean. This metal reflects light in shades of blue and purple.

## Zarinthic Armor
***Magic Armor.*** You have a +2 bonus to your armor class while wearing this armor.

***Zarinthic.*** You can breathe underwater and have a swim speed equal to your walking speed while wearing this armor.

## Zarinthic Weapon
***Magic Weapon.*** You have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

***Zarinthic.*** When you hit a creature with this weapon that is wearing heavy armor, it is [[conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] until the end of its next turn.

***Conductive.*** This weapon can be used as a spellcasting focus.
