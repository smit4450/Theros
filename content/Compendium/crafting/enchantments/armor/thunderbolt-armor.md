# Thunderbolt
*Armor Enchantment, Legendary (requires attunement)*

When you take damage from an attack, you can use a reaction to emit a thunderous boom. Each creature within 30 feet of you must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be [[conditions#Stunned|Stunned]] until the end of your next turn. Once this property is used, it cannot be used again until the next dawn.
