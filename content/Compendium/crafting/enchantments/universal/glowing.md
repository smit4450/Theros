# Glowing
*Universal Enchantment, Uncommon*

While holding or wearing this item, you can take a [[actions#Magic|Magic]] action to make it glow and shed [[bright-light-xphb|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[dim-light-xphb|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. You can use another [[actions#Magic|Magic]] action to make it stop glowing.
