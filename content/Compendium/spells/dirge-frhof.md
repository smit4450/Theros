# Dirge
*6th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Deathly power fills a 60-foot [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]] originating from you for the duration.

When you cast this spell, you can designate creatures to be unaffected by it. Any other creature can't regain [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Points]] while in the [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]]. Whenever the [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]] enters a creature's space and whenever a creature enters the [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]] or ends its turn there, the creature makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes `3d10` Necrotic damage and has the [[conditions#Prone|Prone]] condition. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and its [[speed-xphb|Speed]] is halved. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

## Casting as a Circle Spell

Casting this as a Circle spell requires a minimum of two secondary casters. If the spell is cast as a Circle spell, its duration becomes [[conditions#Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes.

A creature that fails its save against the spell's effect also gains 1 [[conditions#Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level. While the creature has [[conditions#Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] levels, finishing a [[long-rest-xphb|Long Rest]] neither restores lost [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Points]] nor reduces the creature's [[conditions#Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level.

When the spell is cast, each secondary caster must expend a level 4+ spell slot; otherwise, the spell fails.
