# Healing Pools Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 162* 

**Healing Pools Villains**

| dice: d6 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A group of [[returned-palamnite-mot|Returned palamnites]] (see "chapter 6") invade the pools, believing the waters' magic can restore their lost memories and mortal bodies. |
| 2 | [[naiad-mot|Naiads]] (see "chapter 6") guard the pools, making their own judgments about who is and isn't worthy of the pools' healing magic. |
| 3 | The magic waters of the pools wash out to sea and attract a dying [[kraken-legendary-xmm|kraken]] to the area. |
| 4 | An [[assassin-xmm|assassin]] barters captured humanoid test subjects for poison from the pools' [[medusa-xmm|medusa]] priest. |
| 5 | A dying [[warrior-veteran-xmm|veteran]] who was [[conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] by the pools slaughters any person who is healed by the waters. |
| 6 | An [[oracle-npc-mot|oracle]] (see "chapter 6") allows the pools' poisons to seep from the caves and contaminate a community. |
