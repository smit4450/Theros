# Arena Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 134* 

**Arena Villains**

| dice: d8 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A champion pankratiast ([[akroan-hoplite-mot|Akroan hoplite]]; see "chapter 6") has her cronies lock everyone in the arena. She allows none to leave until a challenger defeats her. |
| 2 | A [[minotaur-of-baphomet-xmm|minotaur]] frees all the beasts in the amphitheater during a well-attended event. |
| 3 | A chariot racer ([[gladiator-xmm|gladiator]]) poisons members of a rival team, causing them to fall asleep during competition. |
| 4 | A [[mage-xmm|mage]] uses magic to spy on athletes' conversations and then blackmails them with the information. |
| 5 | A [[stone-giant-xmm|stone giant]] interrupts a session of games and demands a chance to compete. |
| 6 | A promoter forces athletes to overexert themselves, causing dangerous accidents during competitions. |
| 7 | An [[archmage-xmm|archmage]] enchants or polymorphs innocents, forcing them to participate in brutal contests. |
| 8 | A [[druid-xmm|druid]] uses the [[awaken-xphb|awaken]] spell on beasts in the arena to make them more capable competitors. |
