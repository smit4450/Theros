# Thassa's Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 172* 

**Thassa's Villains**

| dice: d6 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A [[triton-master-of-waves-mot|triton master of waves]] (see "chapter 6") demands ever greater sacrifices to Thassa from merchants moving along a crucial trade route, threatening to sink ships that don't comply. |
| 2 | A former [[priest-xmm|priest]] of Thassa kidnaps indigents and plans to drown them as offerings to regain her favor. |
| 3 | A [[druid-xmm|druid]] of Thassa, after being mistreated by a coastal community, uses magic to lure a plague of [[giant-crab-xmm|giant crabs]] out of the depths to take revenge. |
| 4 | A [[water-elemental-xmm|water elemental]] is trapped in a village well and has changed the drinking water to saltwater. It needs help returning to the sea. |
| 5 | Sirens (see the harpies entry in "chapter 6") favored by Thassa take up residence in a lighthouse, preventing its use. |
| 6 | A ship carrying a massive pearl sacred to Thassa has been sunk. Thassa's [[cultist-xmm|cultists]] want the pearl back, but anyone trying to retrieve it find that the god's triton followers have claimed it. |
