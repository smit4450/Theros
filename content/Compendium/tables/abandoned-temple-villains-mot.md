# Abandoned Temple Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 146* 

**Abandoned Temple Villains**

| dice: d6 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A depraved cult of Klothys captures victims on the road and brings them to the temple for ritual torture. |
| 2 | A [[hydra-xmm|hydra]] slumbers within an abandoned temple, having dug a lair out of one of the collapsed sections. |
| 3 | A [[medusa-xmm|medusa]] living in an abandoned temple grants curative aid to those who bring her a humanoid sacrifice. |
| 4 | A [[bandit-captain-xmm|bandit captain]] hiding in a temple hopes to open its vault to find treasure, not knowing that a demon is trapped inside. |
| 5 | [[blood-toll-harpy-mot|Blood-toll harpies]] make an abandoned temple their nest and devour any creatures that get too close. |
| 6 | A [[sphinx-of-lore-xmm|gynosphinx]] curious about human behavior creates a series of deadly traps in an abandoned temple and lures adventurers to the place to witness if and how they overcome its challenges. |
