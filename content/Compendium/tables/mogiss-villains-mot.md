# Mogis's Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 152* 

**Mogis's Villains**

| dice: d8 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A petty tyrant ([[gladiator-xmm|gladiator]]) rules his populace through threats and intimidation. His favorite pastime is forcing unwilling subjects to fight in gladiatorial blood sports. |
| 2 | A band of [[minotaur-of-baphomet-xmm|minotaurs]] directed by a champion of Mogis forges a trail of devastation through the countryside. |
| 3 | A serial killer ([[assassin-xmm|assassin]]) stalks the streets of the polis, taking lives seemingly at random. |
| 4 | An [[oracle-npc-mot|oracle]] (see "chapter 6") of Mogis marches into town and predicts doom on the populace within a fortnight. |
| 5 | Priests of Iroas and Heliod are being murdered by unknown assailants and their temples desecrated. |
| 6 | An [[archmage-xmm|archmage]] is corrupted by Mogis and begins summoning demons to savage the population. |
| 7 | A badly wounded caravan guard ([[bandit-captain-xmm|bandit captain]]) accuses the characters of murder and banditry. |
| 8 | A cabal of Mogis's [[cultist-fanatic-xmm|cult fanatics]] have almost succeeded in finding a way to summon Mogis's warhound to their service. |
