# Amphitheater Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 166* 

**Amphitheater Villains**

| dice: d8 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | An actor charms nobles who see him perform into surrendering their fortunes. |
| 2 | The [[wraith-xmm|wraith]] of a murdered actress reenacts her death every night, playing the role of the murderer and targeting a new performer in her place. |
| 3 | An untalented dancer ([[scout-xmm|scout]]) poisons those with more skill in order to get ahead. |
| 4 | A [[mage-xmm|mage]] playwright invites political figures to see his satires, then uses magic to make them react dramatically, which encourages more people to see his plays. |
| 5 | A leading lady is also an [[assassin-xmm|assassin]] for hire, and she uses a secret room in the theater as her base. |
| 6 | Burglars ([[warrior-veteran-xmm|veterans]]) masquerading as acrobats identify marks during their performances, then rob them afterward. |
| 7 | A murderer ([[spy-xmm|spy]]) who works as a prop maker hides his weapons and victims in among pieces of the set. |
| 8 | A theater-loving [[mage-xmm|mage]] uses a [[homunculus-xmm|homunculus]] to spy on audience members to gain blackmail material. |
