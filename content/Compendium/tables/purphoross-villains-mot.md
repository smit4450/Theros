# Purphoros's Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 168* 

**Purphoros's Villains**

| dice: d6 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A [cult fanatic](Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/cultist-fanatic-xmm.md) of Purphoros turns to Mogis to enhance his craft, engaging in bloody rituals to produce exquisite cursed weapons. |
| 2 | An aging [veteran](Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/warrior-veteran-xmm.md) seeks to replace her body with living bronze and enlists a genius [archmage](Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/archmage-xmm.md) of Purphoros to aid her. Then several of their test limbs escape. |
| 3 | A rebel ([spy](Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/spy-xmm.md)) plots to set fire to a temple of Heliod and dedicate the blaze to Purphoros, driving the gods toward a conflict he's certain Purphoros will win. |
| 4 | A [gladiator](Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/gladiator-xmm.md) of Purphoros partakes of the fumes at Mount Velus then goes on what seems to be a rampage that contributes to a mysterious design. |
| 5 | A [priest](Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/priest-xmm.md) of Purphoros condemns the party for treating their weapons and tools poorly, insisting they discard them until the heroes prove their worthiness. |
| 6 | A group of [oreads](Compendium/bestiary/fey/oread-mot.md) (see "chapter 6") seeks inspiration in flames. The oreads leave Purphoros's forge and conduct thorough studies of everything they burn. |
