# Temple of Mystery Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 150* 

**Temple of Mystery Villains**

| dice: d6 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A bored [[sphinx-of-valor-xmm|androsphinx]] asks visitors riddles with no correct answer, then tears them apart for fun when they respond incorrectly. |
| 2 | A mad [[archmage-xmm|archmage]], obsessed with a mystery she can't solve, wanders the halls of the temple and forces those she meets to confront the puzzle. |
| 3 | A dragon disguised as a humanoid waits in the temple's entrance and demands treasure from anyone leaving. |
| 4 | The [[priest-xmm|priest]] caring for the temple gets jealous whenever adventurers solve a puzzle he couldn't and tries to kill them. |

| 6 | A [[cyclops-sentry-xmm|cyclops]] accidentally trapped in the temple goes on a rampage. |
