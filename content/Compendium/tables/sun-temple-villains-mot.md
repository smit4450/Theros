*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 130* 

**Sun Temple Villains**

| dice: d6 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A once-heroic [[warrior-veteran-xmm|veteran]] seeking to regain his youthful strength tries to burn attendees at a dawn banquet as a sacrifice to Heliod. |
| 2 | A secret cabal of Phenax [[cultist-fanatic-xmm|cult fanatics]] infiltrates the temple and uses details learned in private counseling sessions to blackmail worshipers. |
| 3 | An [[adult-blue-dragon-xmm|adult blue dragon]], scarred by a champion of Heliod, seeks to destroy all sun temples in the hopes of bringing about everlasting night. |
