# Fleshmad Steed
*Source: Theros Bestiary TBVVIII*  

<blockquote><small>More disturbing than the unknown is a distortion of the familiar.</small></blockquote>

<i><b>Fleshmad.</b></i> These six-legged horses are afflicted with a curse of constantly peeling, evaporating, and melting flesh, driving them to madness. To replenish their lost flesh, they must feed on the flesh of humans. Such a feast has a temporary calming effect on the horses.
