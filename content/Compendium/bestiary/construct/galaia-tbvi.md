## Traits

***Charge.*** If Galaia moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a hooves attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

***Faster than Nylea's Arrow.*** Galaia can take the Disengage or Dash action as a bonus action on each of her turns. Galaia ignores difficult terrain, and magical effects can't reduce her speed or cause her to be restrained. She can spend 5 feet of movement to escape from nonmagical restraints or being grappled. When Galaia is prone, standing up only uses 5 feet of her movement.

***Purphoros's Charge.*** Galaia must move on each of its turns. If it discovers the location of the Godsend, it must spend its turns trying to move within 15 feet of Purphoros, whereupon she tells him this information. After that, Galaia may act freely as if she did not have this ability.

***Keen Sight.*** Galaia has Advantage on Wisdom (perception) checks that rely on sight.

***Trample.*** Galaia can move in and out of a Medium or smaller creature's space. If it would, it uses a bonus action to attack that creature with its hooves. That creature must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the creature succeeds, Galaia can't enter that space and must end its turn immediately. If Galaia stops on top of that creature, that creature becomes restrained until Galaia moves off it (escape DC 14).

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** Galaia makes two attacks: one with her hooves and one with her beak.

***Hooves.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one prone creature. Hit: 22 (4d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

***Beak.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d4 + 4) piercing damage.
