## Traits

***Aura of Erebos.*** Any creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the titan must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw, or it takes 10 (3d6) necrotic damage and can’t regain Hit Points until the start of its next turn. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to the titan's Aura of Erebos for 24 hours.

***Magic Resistance.*** The titan has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Siege Monster.*** The titan deals double damage to objects and structures.

***Titanic Nature.*** Unless provoked, the titan ignores all nonflying things that are Huge or smaller and all flying things that are Large or smaller.

## Actions

***Unarmed Strike.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 40 (8d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.

***Noxious Gust (Recharge 5–6).*** The titan exhales a mighty gust that creates a blast of deadly mist in a 60-foot line that is 10 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 36 (8d8) necrotic damage and is knocked prone. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn’t knocked prone.
