## Traits

***Archer's Eye (3/Day).*** As a bonus action, the archer can add 1d10 to its next attack or damage roll with a longbow or shortbow.

***Hearts of the Colossus (Mythic Trait; Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** When the colossus is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it doesn’t die or fall unconscious. Instead, the damage creates tears in its skin, revealing its hearts. The colossus has three hearts in its chest. A heart has an AC of 16 and 165 Hit Points. It is immune to all conditions. If it is forced to make a saving throw, treat its ability scores as 10 (+0). The colossus dies when all the hearts are destroyed.

***Titanic Nature.*** Unless provoked, the giant ignores all nonflying things that are Huge or smaller and all flying things that are Large or smaller.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The colossus makes two attacks with its longbow.

***Longbow.*** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +14 to hit, range 300/1200 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 60 (12d8 + 6) piercing damage.
