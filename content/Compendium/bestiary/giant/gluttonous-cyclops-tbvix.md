## Traits

***Poor Depth Perception.*** The cyclops has Disadvantage on any attack roll against a target more than 30 feet away.

***Hearts of the Cyclops (Mythic Trait; Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** When the cyclops is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it doesn’t die or fall unconscious. Instead, the damage creates tears in its skin, revealing its hearts. The cyclops has three hearts in its chest. A heart has an AC of 14 and 145 Hit Points. It is immune to all conditions. If it is forced to make a saving throw, treat its ability scores as 10 (+0). The cyclops dies when all the hearts are destroyed.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The cyclops makes two greatclub attacks.

***Greatclub.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 48 (10d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

***Rock.*** Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 55 (10d10) bludgeoning damage.
