## Traits

***Inspired.*** At the beginning of the worshiper's turn, if a bird the worshiper bowed to at any point since the worshiper's last turn saw it doing so, that bird uses a bonus action to summons a **nyxborn eagle** that appears in an unoccupied space that the worshiper can see within 60 feet of itself. The summoned eagle acts as an ally to its summoner.
