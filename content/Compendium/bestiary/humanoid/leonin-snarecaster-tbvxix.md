***Daunting Roar (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** As a bonus action, the leonin can let out an especially menacing roar. Creatures of it chooses within 10 feet of itself that can hear it must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of it until the end of your next turn.
