***Enchantment Incentive.*** Any creature that has not cast an enchantment from within 30 feet of the hero since that creature's last long rest may do so without expending a spell slot.

***Heroic.*** Whenever the hero is the target of a spell, that spell's caster chooses whether following happens: - Until the end of combat, the hero gains a +1 bonus to damage rolls and Dexterity checks, and it gains 4 (1d8) temporary Hit Points.
