***Hold the Line.*** While the sergeant is holding a sword, other creatures provoke an opportunity attack from the sergeant when they move within 5 feet of it. ***Unrenowned.*** The sergeant has a -1 penalty on damage rolls and Strength and Dexterity checks, and its hit point maximum is reduced by 4 (1d8). The first time the sergeant deals damage to a player character, it loses this trait permanently.
