## Traits

***Charge.*** If the emissary moves at least 20 ft. straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

***Magic Weapons.*** The emissary's weapon attacks are magical.

***Spell Nature.*** In addition to being a creature, the emissary is a 4th-level divine transmutation spell with no target.

***Starlight Form.*** The emissary glows with the soft light of the night sky, shedding dim light in a 15-foot radius.

***Telepathic Bond.*** While the emissary is in any of Theros's three realms, it can magically convey what it senses to Heliod.
