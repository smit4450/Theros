## Traits

***Amphibious.*** The emissary can breathe air and water.

***Magic Weapons.*** The emissary's weapon attacks are magical.

***Spell Nature.*** In addition to being a creature, the emissary is a 4th-level divine transmutation spell with no target.

***Starlight Form.*** The emissary glows with the soft light of the night sky, shedding dim light in a 15-foot radius.

***Telepathic Bond.*** While the emissary is in any of Theros's three realms, it can magically convey what it senses to Thassa.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The emissary makes two attacks with its claws.

***Claw.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 20 (3d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 15). The emissary has two claws, each of which can grapple only one target.
