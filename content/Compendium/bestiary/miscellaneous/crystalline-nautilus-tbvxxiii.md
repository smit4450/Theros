***Glass Shell.*** If the nautilus takes 100 or more damage in a single turn, its shell shatters, and it dies instantly.

***Magic Weapons.*** The nautilus's weapon attacks are magical.

***Primitive Vision.*** The nautilus's eyes are useful for detecting light, and not much else.

***Spell Nature.*** In addition to being a creature, the nautilus is a 3rd-level divine transmutation spell with no target.

***Starlight Form.*** The nautilus glows with the soft light of the night sky, shedding dim light in a 15-foot radius.

***Telepathic Bond.*** While the nautilus is in any of Theros's three realms, it can magically convey what it senses to Thassa.

***Water Breathing.*** The nautilus can breathe only underwater.
