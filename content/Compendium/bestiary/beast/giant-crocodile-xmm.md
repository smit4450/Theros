## Traits

***Hold Breath.*** The crocodile can hold its breath for 1 hour.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The crocodile makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* `+8`, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 21 (`3d10 + 5`) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [Grappled](/Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Grappled) condition (escape DC 15). While [Grappled](/Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Grappled), the target has the [Restrained](/Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) condition and can't be targeted by the crocodile's Tail.

***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* `+8`, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (`3d8 + 5`) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [Prone](/Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Prone) condition.
