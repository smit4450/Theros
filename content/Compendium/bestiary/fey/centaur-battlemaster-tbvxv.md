## Traits

***Battle Readiness.*** The warrior has Advantage on initiative rolls.

***Charge.*** If the warrior moves at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a melee attack on the same turn, it can immediately follow that attack with a bonus action, making one attack against the target with its hooves.

***Equine Build.*** The warrior counts as one size larger when determining its carrying capacity and the weight it can push or drag. In addition, any climb that requires hands and feet is especially difficult for it because of its equine legs. When it makes such a climb, each foot of movement costs it 4 extra feet instead of the normal 1 extra foot.

***Extra Attack.*** The warrior can attack twice, instead of once, whenever it takes the Attack action on its turn.

***Improved Critical.*** The warrior's attack rolls score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The battlemaster makes two attacks with its scimitar.

***Scimitar.*** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.

***Hooves.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

## Reactions

***Heroic.*** Whenever the battlemaster becomes targeted by a spell, that spell's caster chooses whether the following happens: - Until the end of combat, the battlemaster gains a +1 bonus to damage rolls and Strength checks, and it gains 4 (1d8) temporary Hit Points.
