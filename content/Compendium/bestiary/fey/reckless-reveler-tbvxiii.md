## Traits

***Careless Ramming.*** While the satyr bears a lit torch, its ram attacks deal an extra 4 (1d8) fire damage per lit torch.

***Charge.*** If the celebrant moves at least 15 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with its ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 5 (2d4) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

***Magic Resistance.*** The satyr has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Mirthful Leaps.*** Whenever the celebrant makes a long or high jump, it can cover an additional 1d8 feet, even when making a standing jump. This extra distance costs movement as normal.

***Siege Monster.*** The celebrant deals double damage to objects and structures.

***Sleepless Reveler.*** Magic can’t put the satyr to sleep.

***Hedonism.*** As a bonus action, the reveler can ignite itself. Any objects within 5 feet also ignite, and the reveler drops to 0 Hit Points.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The satyr makes two ram attacks or two torch attacks.

***Ram.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (2d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

***Torch.*** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d8 + 3) fire damage.
