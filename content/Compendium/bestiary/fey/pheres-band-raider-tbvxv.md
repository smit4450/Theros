## Traits

***Battle Readiness.*** The centaur has Advantage on initiative rolls.

***Charge.*** If the centaur moves at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a melee attack on the same turn, it can immediately follow that attack with a bonus action, making one attack against the target with its hooves.

***Equine Build.*** The centaur counts as one size larger when determining its carrying capacity and the weight it can push or drag. In addition, any climb that requires hands and feet is especially difficult for it because of its equine legs. When it makes such a climb, each foot of movement costs it 4 extra feet instead of the normal 1 extra foot.

***Extra Attack.*** The centaur can attack twice, instead of once, whenever it takes the Attack action on its turn.

***Improved Critical.*** The centaur's attack rolls score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.

## Actions

***Hooves.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, one target. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

***Shield Bash.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

***Spear.*** _Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage in melee, or 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage at range.

***Bow.*** The centaur bows down to any manifestation or statue of Kruphix that it can see. It remains in this position until a different action is used.

## Reactions

***Inspired.*** At the beginning of the centaur's turn, if Kruphix saw it bowing to him or an image of him at any point since the centaur's last turn, Kruphix uses a bonus action to summon two **nyxborn centaurs** that appear in unoccupied spaces that the centaur can see within 60 feet of itself. The summoned centaurs act as allies to their summoner and to each other.
