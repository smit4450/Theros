## Traits

***Battle Readiness.*** The centaur has Advantage on initiative rolls.

***Charge.*** If the centaur moves at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a melee attack on the same turn, it can immediately follow that attack with a bonus action, making one attack against the target with its hooves.

***Equine Build.*** The centaur counts as one size larger when determining its carrying capacity and the weight it can push or drag. In addition, any climb that requires hands and feet is especially difficult for it because of its equine legs. When it makes such a climb, each foot of movement costs it 4 extra feet instead of the normal 1 extra foot.

***Extra Attack.*** The centaur can attack twice, instead of once, whenever it takes the Attack action on its turn.

***Improved Critical.*** The centaur's attack rolls score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.

## Actions

***Hooves.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, one target. Hit: 4 (1d4+2) bludgeoning damage.
