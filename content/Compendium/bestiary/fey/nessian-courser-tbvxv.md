## Traits

***Battle Readiness.*** The courser has Advantage on initiative rolls.

***Charge.*** If the courser moves at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a melee attack on the same turn, it can immediately follow that attack with a bonus action, making one attack against the target with its hooves.

***Equine Build.*** The courser counts as one size larger when determining its carrying capacity and the weight it can push or drag. In addition, any climb that requires hands and feet is especially difficult for it because of its equine legs. When it makes such a climb, each foot of movement costs it 4 extra feet instead of the normal 1 extra foot.

***Improved Critical.*** The courser's attack rolls score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The courser makes two attacks.

***Hooves.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

***Glaive.*** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage.

***Longbow.*** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.
