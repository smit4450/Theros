# Entertainer
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 180*  
![](Compendium/backgrounds/img/entertainer.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores.** Strength, Dexterity, Charisma  
- **Feat.** [[musician-xphb|Musician]]  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [[skills#Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[skills#Performance|Performance]]  
- **Tool Proficiency.** Choose one kind of [[musical-instrument-xphb|Musical Instrument]]  
- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [[musical-instrument-xphb|Musical Instrument]] (same as above), [[costume-xphb|2 Costumes]], [[mirror-xphb|Mirror]], [[perfume-xphb|Perfume]], [[travelers-clothes-xphb|Traveler's Clothes]], 11 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

You spent much of your youth following roving fairs and carnivals, performing odd jobs for musicians and acrobats in exchange for lessons. You may have learned how to walk a tightrope, how to play a [[lute-xphb|lute]] in a distinct style, or how to recite poetry with impeccable diction. To this day, you thrive on applause and long for the stage.
