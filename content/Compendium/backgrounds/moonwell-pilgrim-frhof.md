# Moonwell Pilgrim
*Source: FRHoF p. 33*  
![](Compendium/backgrounds/img/moonwell-pilgrim.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores.** Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma  
- **Feat.** [[magic-initiate-xphb|Magic Initiate]] (Druid)  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [[skills#Nature|Nature]] and [[skills#Performance|Performance]]  
- **Tool Proficiencies.** [[painters-supplies-xphb|Painter's Supplies]]  
- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [[quarterstaff-xphb|Quarterstaff]], [[painters-supplies-xphb|Painter's Supplies]], [[bedroll-xphb|Bedroll]], [[bell-xphb|Bell]], [[pouch-xphb|Pouch]], [[robe-xphb|Robe]], [[string-xphb|String]], [[travelers-clothes-xphb|Traveler's Clothes]], [[waterskin-xphb|Waterskin]], 38 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

Like many who hail from the Moonshae Isles, you grew up revering the blessed land, its unique gods, and the mysterious shrines called the moonwells. As a moonwell pilgrim, you undertook a quest to visit and commune with every moonwell on (or off) the map. Along your idyllic journeys, you collected a repertoire of Moonshavian folk songs, painted landscapes of enchanting vistas, and even learned how to wield a bit of primal magic.
