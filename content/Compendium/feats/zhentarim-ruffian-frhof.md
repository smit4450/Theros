# Zhentarim Ruffian
*Source: FRHoF p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Exploit Opening.** When you roll damage for an [[actions#Opportunity%20Attack|Opportunity Attack]], you can roll the damage dice twice and use either roll against the target.

**Family First.** If you have [[heroic-inspiration-xphb|Heroic Inspiration]] when you roll [[initiative-xphb|Initiative]], you can expend it to give yourself and your allies [[advantage-xphb|Advantage]] on that [[initiative-xphb|Initiative]] roll.
