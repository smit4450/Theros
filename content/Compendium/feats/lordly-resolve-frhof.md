# Lordly Resolve
*Source: FRHoF p. 40*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Standard Bearer.** As a [[bonus-action-xphb|Bonus Action]], choose up to three creatures within 60 feet of yourself that can see you. Each target can immediately take a [[reaction-xphb|Reaction]] to right itself and end the [[conditions#Prone|Prone]] condition, provided its [[speed-xphb|Speed]] isn't 0. Additionally, you bolster the targets' resolve, which lasts for 1 minute or until you have the [[conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition. While bolstered, a target can't be possessed or gain the [[conditions#Charmed|Charmed]] or [[conditions#Frightened|Frightened]] condition; if a target is already possessed, [[conditions#Charmed|Charmed]], or [[conditions#Frightened|Frightened]], the target has [[advantage-xphb|Advantage]] on any new saving throw against the relevant effect.

Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a [[long-rest-xphb|Long Rest]].
