# Slasher
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 208*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Hamstring.** Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack that deals Slashing damage, you can reduce the [[speed-xphb|Speed]] of that creature by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**Enhanced Critical.** When you score a [[critical-hit-xphb|Critical Hit]] that deals Slashing damage to a creature, it has [[disadvantage-xphb|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls until the start of your next turn.
