# Boon of the Soul Drinker
*Source: FRHoF p. 43*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Grave Resistance.** You have [[resistance-rules-xphb|Resistance]] to Cold damage and Necrotic damage.

**Siphon Life.** When an enemy within 120 feet of you is reduced to 0 [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Points]], you can take a [[reaction-xphb|Reaction]] to regain 50 [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Points]]. Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a [[short-rest-xphb|Short]] or [[long-rest-xphb|Long Rest]].
