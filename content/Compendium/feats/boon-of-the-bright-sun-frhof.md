# Boon of the Bright Sun
*Source: FRHoF p. 43*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Constitution, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Daylight Presence.** As a [[bonus-action-xphb|Bonus Action]], you radiate a 30-foot [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]] of [[bright-light-xphb|Bright Light]] that is sunlight. If any of the [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]]'s area overlaps with an area of [[darkness-rules-xphb|Darkness]] created by a spell, that spell is dispelled. The [[emanation-area-of-effect-xphb|Emanation]] lasts until you dismiss it (no action required), die, or have the [[conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.

**Fortifying Light.** When your Daylight Presence is active, at the start of each of your turns, you and allies you can see in your Daylight Presence gain 10 [[temporary-hit-points-xphb|Temporary Hit Points]].
