# Order's Resilience
*Source: FRHoF p. 40*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Resurge.** When you have the [[conditions#Prone|Prone]] condition, you can right yourself with only 5 feet of movement.

**Stronger Together.** If you are within 5 feet of an ally that doesn't have the [[conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition, you and that ally have [[advantage-xphb|Advantage]] on Strength saving throws. You can't use this benefit while you have the [[conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
