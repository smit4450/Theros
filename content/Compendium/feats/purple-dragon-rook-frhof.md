# Purple Dragon Rook
*Source: FRHoF p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Entreat.** You gain proficiency in one of the following skills: [[skills#Insight|Insight]], [[skills#Performance|Performance]], or [[skills#Persuasion|Persuasion]].

**Rallying Cry.** When you roll [[initiative-xphb|Initiative]] and don't have the [[conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition, you can choose a number of creatures equal to your [[proficiency-xphb|Proficiency Bonus]] that you can see within 30 feet of yourself. Those creatures gain [[heroic-inspiration-xphb|Heroic Inspiration]].

Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a [[long-rest-xphb|Long Rest]].
