# Boon of Fortitude
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 210*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Fortified Health.** Your [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Point]] maximum increases by 40. In addition, whenever you regain [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Points]], you can regain additional [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Points]] equal to your Constitution modifier. Once you've regained these additional [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Points]], you can't do so again until the start of your next turn.
