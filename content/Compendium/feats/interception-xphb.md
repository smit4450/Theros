# Interception
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 209*  

**Prerequisite**: Fighting Style Feature

When a creature you can see hits another creature within 5 feet of you with an attack roll, you can take a [[reaction-xphb|Reaction]] to reduce the damage dealt to the target by `1d10` plus your [[proficiency-xphb|Proficiency Bonus]]. You must be holding a [[shield-spell-xphb|Shield]] or a Simple or Martial weapon to use this [[reaction-xphb|Reaction]].
