# Spellfire Spark
*Source: FRHoF p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Magic Absorption.** Once per turn, when you take damage from a spell or magical effect, you reduce the total damage taken by `1d4`. You can't use this benefit if you have the [[conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.

**Spellfire Flame.** You learn the [[sacred-flame-xphb|Sacred Flame]] cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat). You can also cast this cantrip as a [[bonus-action-xphb|Bonus Action]] a number of times equal to your [[proficiency-xphb|Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[long-rest-xphb|Long Rest]].
