# Boon of Bloodshed
*Source: FRHoF p. 42*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Killer's Fortune.** When an enemy you can see is reduced to 0 [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Points]], you gain [[advantage-xphb|Advantage]] on the next attack roll you make before the end of your next turn.

**Power from Pain.** Once per turn, when you make an attack roll while [[conditions#Bloodied|Bloodied]], you can deal extra damage to the target equal to your [[proficiency-xphb|Proficiency Bonus]]. The extra damage's type is the same as the attack's type.
