# Purple Dragon Commandant
*Source: FRHoF p. 40*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th or Proficiency with martial weapons

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Encourage Ally.** As a [[bonus-action-xphb|Bonus Action]], you bolster one ally you can see within 30 feet. The ally gains [[temporary-hit-points-xphb|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to `2d6` plus the modifier of the ability score increased by this feat. You can take this [[bonus-action-xphb|Bonus Action]] a number of times equal to your [[proficiency-xphb|Proficiency Bonus]], and you regain all uses when you finish a [[long-rest-xphb|Long Rest]].

**Last Stand.** You have [[advantage-xphb|Advantage]] on attack rolls while [[conditions#Bloodied|Bloodied]].
