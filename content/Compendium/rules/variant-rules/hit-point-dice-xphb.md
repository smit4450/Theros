# Hit Point Dice
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 368. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

Hit Point Dice, or Hit Dice for short, help determine a [[player-character-xphb|player character's]] Hit Point maximum, as explained in "chapter 2". Most [[monster-xphb|monsters]] also have Hit Dice. A creature can spend Hit Dice during a [[short-rest-xphb|Short Rest]] to regain [[hit-points-xphb|Hit Points]].
